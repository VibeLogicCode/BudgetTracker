# Whole-app review after v1.48.0 — 2026-09-19

Seven independent read-only passes over `fecda29` (v1.48.0): data integrity, wrong reporting,
performance, security and authorization, the remaining domains (categorization, SimpleFIN, bills,
receipts, goals, accounts, transactions, dates), and a two-assessment design critique. Every P0 and
P1 below was re-verified against the code by the coordinating session before this file was written.
Line numbers are from the working tree at `fecda29`.

Severity: **P0** wrong money shown or exploitable · **P1** silent wrong state, data loss, or two
surfaces disagreeing · **P2** edge case with real consequences · **P3** hygiene.

The fix plan is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-19-v1.48.1-fix-release.md`.

## A. Data integrity (loan ledger)

### A1 — P1 — Adjustment rows are dated "today" but the anchor wall filters on `period_end`
`src/lib/loans/ledger.ts:452-455` dates every correction `periodStart = periodEnd = today`. Both
readers that decide "what is after the anchor" filter `period_end > anchor.as_of_date` with no
`kind` filter: `recomputeBalance` at `src/lib/loans.ts:523-531` and `ledgerFacts` at `:967`.

Repro: loan anchored 1 Jan, monthly postings. On 5 Apr a payment dated 10 Feb is linked, writing an
adjustment with `period_end = 2026-04-05` correcting February. On 10 Apr the household reconciles a
statement dated 1 Apr. `2026-04-05 > 2026-04-01`, so the February correction is re-applied on top of
a statement that already contains it. Wrong by the adjustment amount, permanently.

Fix: the wall must select postings by the period they belong to, not by their write date. Filter
stored postings and the balance replay on `period_start >= anchor.as_of_date` (a posting or
adjustment whose period began before the statement is superseded by it), and give an adjustment a
`period_start` equal to the start of the earliest period it corrects. A period that straddles the
anchor is then recomputed from the anchor as a short first period, which is what C2 intended.

### A2 — P1 — `setLoanAnchor` commits a balance it knows is wrong; the correction runs post-commit and is swallowed
`src/lib/loans.ts:800-820`. Inside the transaction `recomputeBalance` adds the **full** interest of
any stored posting whose `period_end` is after a mid-cycle statement date. The correction only runs
afterwards via `postAfterChange` (`:1178-1184`), which catches and logs.

Repro: posting day 1, postings through 1 Apr. On 5 Apr reconcile a statement dated 15 Mar. The
short period 15 Mar → 1 Apr has an end already in `storedEnds`, so nothing is due; meanwhile the
whole 1 Mar → 1 Apr charge was added to the confirmed figure. Committed state = statement + almost
a month of interest the statement superseded.

Fix: make reconcile atomic. `postDueInterest` takes an optional transaction handle (better-sqlite3
nests via savepoints; the "nesting would deadlock" note at `:1173-1176` is wrong for this driver)
and runs inside `setLoanAnchor`'s transaction. With A1's period-start wall the straddling posting is
excluded rather than added whole.

### A3 — P1 — Statement dates are shape-validated only; the newest anchor wins; nothing can be retracted
`src/app/(app)/warranties/actions.ts:524-525` and `src/lib/warranty/items.ts:309-313` check
`/^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$/` and nothing else. The effective anchor is `order by as_of_date desc`
(`src/lib/loans.ts:485-491`, `:920-926`). No delete or retract path exists for `loan_anchors`.

Repro: type `2027-06-01` for a statement dated `2026-06-01`. Every payment is zeroed by the wall,
`debtOverTime` marks every month unknown, and no later anchor can win because its date is earlier.

Fix: refuse an as-of date after today or before the loan's start date in both the reconcile action
and the item schema. Add a `retracted_at` column that both `order by` queries filter on, with a
"Withdraw this statement" action on the anchor history.

### A4 — P1 — `buildLedger` throws on the render path of four pages and aborts the whole nightly sweep
`src/lib/loans/ledger.ts:111-119` throws after 1200 periods; `:59-69`/`:80-90` on unreachable
dates. `listLoans → interestFor → loanLedger` has no try/catch (`src/lib/loans.ts:2800`) and is
called from dashboard, reports, transactions and the warranty detail page. `postAllDueInterest`
loops with no per-item isolation (`:1160-1165`).

Fix: per-item try/catch in the sweep, counted into the result; `interestFor` degrades to
`{ interest: null }` on a throw; a typed `LedgerRangeError`; and A3 stops the bad date getting in.

### A5 — P2 — Per-period clamp in `computePeriods` vs single clamp in `replayedAtOpen` manufactures adjustments
`ledger.ts:377` clamps each period's closing at zero; `:432-436` clamps the replay once. Any amount
a per-period clamp swallowed appears as a non-zero gap and is written as an "interest" adjustment.
Reachable because `recomputeBalance` front-loads all posted interest before walking payments.

Fix: one clamping rule. Replay the stored rows per period with the same clamp, or have
`computePeriods` return the clamped-away total and subtract it from the gap.

### A6 — P2 — `updateWarrantyItem` writes the raw balance outside any transaction, rate change before anchor, anchor failure swallowed
`src/lib/warranty/items.ts:695-712` bare update; `addRateChange` (`:720-735`) then `setLoanAnchor`
(`:746-765`) each in their own transaction; the anchor's catch returns success. `addRateChange`
posts closed periods against the OLD anchor at the new rate before the new anchor exists.

Fix: anchor first, then rate change, ideally all inside one transaction with handles passed
through; do not swallow the anchor failure. Extend guard G3 to scan `items.ts`.

### A7 — P2 — The rate-history fallback substitutes `0` for a missing rate
`src/lib/loans.ts:995-1002`: `rateBps ?? 0` when history is empty. A missing rate becomes a positive
claim of "interest-free" written into closed periods forever. The fallback itself (item columns as
the current truth, dated at the anchor) is consistent with 0026's backfill and stays; the `?? 0`
must return no ledger instead.

### A8 — P2 — Transaction delete and import undo move the balance by arithmetic and never catch the ledger up
`src/lib/loans.ts:2173-2179` and `:2100-2107` do `set current = max(0, current + restore)`. The two
delete paths (`src/lib/transactions.ts:1376-1378`, `src/lib/import/commit.ts:565, 597`) never call
`postAfterChange`. Postings still count the deleted payment until the 02:00 sweep, which per A4 may
not be running.

Fix: `reverseLoanLinksForTransactions` returns the distinct item ids; both callers post after
commit; replace the arithmetic restore with `recomputeBalance`.

### A9 — P3 — Debt chart interest series counts adjustments
`src/lib/loans.ts:3005-3013`. Correct once A1/A5 stop producing spurious adjustments (a correction
is a net change to interest charged); revisit only if it reads wrong after that.

### A10 — P3 — Statement receipt is stored before the anchor; a failed anchor orphans it
`src/app/(app)/warranties/actions.ts:552-583`. Write the anchor first, or delete the receipt in the
failure branch.

### A11 — P3 — A backdated reconcile computes `previous` as the newest anchor by date
`src/lib/loans.ts:753-771`: entering an older statement after a newer one yields an empty
`movementsBetween` and a meaningless `difference_cents`, and moves `balance_updated_at` backwards.
Compute `previous` as the newest anchor strictly before the new date; skip the comparison columns.

**Correct and worth knowing:** FK enforcement is genuinely on with `foreign_key_check` at boot; all
new tables cascade from `warranty_items`; backup (`VACUUM INTO` + rename) and restore need no table
list; a half-migrated schema is unreachable (one transaction, exit before serve); both delete paths
reverse loan links before deleting and a guard pins that; posting rows are race-safe under the
partial unique index.

## B. Wrong reporting

### B1 — P0 — `payoffProjection` charges a daily rate once per month for `apr_daily`
`src/lib/loans.ts:2564-2579`. `ratePpb(bps, 'apr_daily')` is per-day; the loop charges it once per
month-iteration. $20,000 at 19.99% with $350/month projects "October 2031"; truth is that the
balance barely moves. Rendered on the dashboard (`src/components/LoansCard.tsx:89-91`) and the loan
page. From v1.47.0.

Fix: dispatch on basis as `ledger.ts:695` does (365 daily charges per iteration for `apr_daily`, or
convert to a monthly-equivalent ppb), and pin a test against the same loan's
`ledger.interestThisPeriodCents`.

### B2 — P0 — `interestFor.potCents` is exactly 2 × accrued; `interestSinceAnchorCents` double-counts paid interest
`src/lib/loans.ts:2742-2747`. `owing − posted + accrued` ≡ `2 × accrued`. Neither field is rendered
today. Fix: add `interestPostedSinceStartCents` to `Ledger`; `pot = max(0, posted − paid) +
accrued`; `since = posted + accrued`. Or delete both fields.

### B3 — P0 — `interestPaidToDateCents` / `principalPaidToDateCents` ignore arrears
`src/lib/loans/ledger.ts:563-565`: `min(payments, thisPeriodInterest)`. June unpaid, July paid
$500: card says "Paid in interest $100.84 / Paid off the loan $399.16" while the balance moved
$299.16. Fix: carry an `unpaidInterest` accumulator across closed periods.

### B4 — P0 — The same two figures exclude the open period
`ledger.ts:534-538, 607-609`. Payments in the current cycle appear as rows but sum into neither
stat. Fix: after the closed loop, split open-period payments against accrued-so-far plus carried
arrears.

### B5 — P0 — Three zero-clamp models give three "Balance" figures for one loan
Row walk clamps per movement (`ledger.ts:646-653`); `computePeriods` clamps per period (`:377`);
`recomputeBalance` front-loads posted interest then clamps chronologically
(`src/lib/loans.ts:531, 554`). Reproduced: MetricCard "Balance $0.00", ledger "Owing today $2.03 /
Balance $2.03", dashboard $0.00, and `announceIfPaidOff` fires. Fix: carry a signed running balance
through the descriptors and clamp only at period boundaries, so `postedBalanceCents` is by
construction the last closing plus open movements; pin `postedBalanceCents === current_balance_cents`.

### B6 — P1 — Dashboard "What we owe" is the stored balance; the loan page headline is owing-today
`src/app/(app)/dashboard/page.tsx:206` sums `currentBalanceCents`; `loansTotalOwedCents`
(`src/lib/loans.ts:2836-2851`) exists to fix exactly this and has no callers in `src/`;
`netWorthOverTime` takes debt from `debtOverTime`. Fix: one quantity for "what we owe" routed
through every surface, and a test that the dashboard total equals the sum of each loan's headline.

### B7 — P1 — `loan_payment_missed` reads a frozen `payments_cents`
`src/lib/notify/evaluate/loans.ts:177-192`. Postings are `onConflictDoNothing`, so a payment
imported after the period closed never updates the column; the ledger card recomputes it from facts
and shows the payment while the notification says none was recorded. Fix: query `loan_payments ⋈
transactions` for the period window (the `movementsBetween` predicate).

### B8 — P2 — The Interest column shows the same period's interest twice
Payment rows carry `interestCents = accruedToDay` (`ledger.ts:498-519`), a subset of the posting
row's figure; summing the column over-counts, and the CSV exports it as "Interest". Fix: move
accrued-to-day into `detail`, leave `interestCents` null on payment rows, add a separate CSV column.

### B9 — P2 — `cashflowTrend`'s default `endMonth` is UTC
`src/lib/reports.ts:179`: the only `toISOString().slice(0, 10)` left in `src/`. Unreachable today
(all callers pass `endMonth`). Fix: `monthOf(todayIso())` plus an ops grep guard.

### B10 — P3 — "in interest this month" states a cycle projection as a monthly fact
`src/components/LoansCard.tsx:37, 55-59` vs the ledger card's "This cycle" and the row chip's
"est.". Match the labels.

**Correct and worth knowing:** `NOT_PRINCIPAL_MOVEMENT`'s quantifier; the anchor wall is consistent
between engine and writer (before A1's fix is applied, both agree); viewer scoping on every reports
and budgets aggregate; `budgetTotals` overlap rule; date boundaries uniformly half-open.

## C. Performance

One dashboard render at 40 categories / 10 loans / 3 years / 20k transactions: ~400–550 SQLite
statements, 30–90 ms server work on a dev box (a NAS is 2–4× slower).

### C1 — P1 — `listLoans` builds every loan's full ledger; three pages call it for data that needs none of it
`src/lib/loans.ts:2761 → interestFor → loanLedger`, plus `payoffProjection`, per loan row. Callers
that discard it: `transactions/page.tsx:175` (a dropdown), `reports/page.tsx:202` (two booleans),
`warranties/[id]/page.tsx:32` (unconditionally, for every item kind). ~100 queries + 13–20 ms on the
most-visited page. Fix: `listLoanRows` (cheap base query) and `listLoanSummaries` (with derived
figures); point the three callers at the cheap one.

### C2 — P1 — The loan detail page builds the same ledger three times and every other loan's once
`warranties/[id]/page.tsx:32` and `:68`; `interestFor` reads movements twice. ~105 queries, 11
`buildLedger` runs for one loan. Fix: `getWarrantyItem` first; only for a loan call a new
`loanDetail(itemId, today)` that reads `ledgerFacts` once and derives everything from it.

### C3 — P1 — `budgetProgress` is a per-category N+1, run two or three times per dashboard load
`src/lib/budgets.ts:567 → buildRow → effectiveBudget → resolveBudget + rolloverStartMonth`: two
queries per category; `categorySpendWithRollupSeries` (a 24-month GROUP BY) re-run per rollover
category. Called at `dashboard/page.tsx:89`, inside `safeToSpend` (`bills.ts:199`), and again at
`:231`. Fix: batch the per-category lookups into two maps per call; run the 24-month series once
across all categories; memoize `budgetProgress` per request with `React.cache`.

### C4 — P1 — `buildLedger` calls `periodCharge` once per movement (38–74% of engine time)
`ledger.ts:498`. Measured 1.30 ms → 0.80 ms (36 movements), 5.48 → 1.42 ms (300 movements). Fix:
compute each period's day-balance array and running charge prefix once in `computePeriods`; index
by `daysBetweenIso(start, movement.date)`.

### C5 — P1 — Dashboard savings chart recomputes 12 monthly aggregates that `cashflowTrend(12)` just produced
`dashboard/page.tsx:112` maps `savingsProgress(row.month)` over the trend; each calls
`cashflowTrend(1)` again. ~40 queries. Fix: `savingsTargetsFor(months[])` + derive from the trend row.

### C6 — P2 — `/reports` computes `debtOverTime(24)` twice
`reports/page.tsx:225` and inside `netWorthOverTime` (`networth.ts:338`). Accept a precomputed
series or memoize per request.

### C7 — P2 — The two loan notify evaluators run every 5-minute tick, not once per slot, with a query per loan
`src/lib/notify/evaluate/index.ts:207-208` sit outside the `lastDailyEvaluatedSlot` memo at `:214`.
`loans.ts:170` re-selects `expiryDate` already selected by `loansFor`. Fix: move both inside the
slot memo; add `expiryDate` to `loansFor`; collapse the per-loan probes into one grouped query.

### C8 — P2 — `alreadyAnnounced` scans the whole outbox; no index on `event_id`
`src/lib/notify/evaluate/loans.ts:37`. Measured 704 µs at 6k rows → 466 µs with a covering index.
Fix: `index(event_id, dedup_key)`, and bound the query by `created_at`.

### C9 — P2 — `postAllDueInterest` opens one transaction per loan, each fsyncing
`src/lib/loans.ts:1148`. Fix: one transaction around the sweep (pairs with A2's handle).
`PRAGMA synchronous` is unset (FULL); NORMAL in WAL mode is the standard pairing but is a
durability decision, recorded here, not made.

### C10 — P2 — `link()` re-replays the whole loan on every linked row: O(K²) on import/backfill
`src/lib/loans.ts:642`. Fix: in `applyPaymentMatchers` and `backfillLoanRule`, insert all rows then
call `recomputeBalance` once.

### C11 — P2 — `debtOverTime` aggregates all history for any window; the interest fold is O(months × postings)
`src/lib/loans.ts:2985, 3010, 3070`. Add window bounds plus a pre-window total per item; build the
cumulative map in one pass.

### C12 — P3 — `warranty-detail-client.tsx` re-renders the whole ledger on any state change
1,575-line client component, nothing memoized, `collapseToPeriods` recomputed per render, 60–100 KB
flight payload for a long line of credit. Fix: `React.memo` the card and the linked table,
`useMemo` the collapse, cap the expanded ledger at ~100 rows with "show all".

**Missing indexes:** `notification_outbox(event_id, dedup_key)`; `budget_rollover(scope, user_id,
category_id)`; `loan_payments(source, created_at)`; partial `warranty_items(interest_rate_basis)`.

**Already efficient, leave alone:** `debtOverTime`'s three-query shape; `NOT_PRINCIPAL_MOVEMENT` as a
correlated `not exists`; the 400-id chunking; the dormancy bails (the idle tick is negligible);
`listTransactions`'s single count+sums statement; the dashboard deliberately not calling
`loansTotalOwedCents` twice.

## D. Security and authorization

25 API route handlers and ~130 server actions enumerated. Origin and auth coverage is near-total.
The gaps concentrate in the loan-link write paths and the budget household-scope branch.

### D1 — P0 — `applySuggestionAction` hands a self-visibility member the household's spending figures
`src/app/(app)/budgets/actions.ts:147-188`. The MUST-7.6 check at `:168-171` fires only for
`scope === 'personal'`; `scope=household&userId=` passes, `suggestionsFor` computes from the whole
household's history, and `:187` returns the figure in the message. Iterating `categoryId`
enumerates the household's per-category spend. `tests/ops/visibility-invariants.test.ts`'s
`HOUSEHOLD_ONLY_AT_PAGE` reason string ("never a suggestion figure") is false.

Fix: refuse `isSelfScoped(user) && scope === 'household'` before `suggestionsFor` is called;
correct the guard's reason string; add a test as a self member asserting refusal and no write.

### D2 — P1 — `unassignFromLoanAction` reads the ownership gate and throws it away
`src/app/(app)/transactions/actions.ts:989`: `getTransaction(id, user)` is called and its null
result never checked. Fix: `if (txn === null) return { error: NOT_YOURS_ERROR };`.

### D3 — P1 — `assignToLoanAction` gates neither the transaction nor the loan item
`src/app/(app)/transactions/actions.ts:808-827`. Fix: resolve both through the viewer before the
write; hoist the item read at `:893` above it.

### D4 — P1 — `unlinkRulePaymentAction` discards its session and checks nothing
`src/app/(app)/dashboard/actions.ts:218-235`. Same write as the guarded
`unlinkLedgerTransactionAction`. Fix: bind the user; gate on `getWarrantyItem(itemId, user)`.
Longer term: `assignTransactionToLoan`, `unassignTransactionFromLoan`, `unlinkItemTransaction` take
a required `viewer` and join `REQUIRE_VIEWER`.

### D5 — P2 — Household-scope budget writes are open to a member the UI hides that scope from
`budgets/actions.ts:30, 64, 106, 195`. Decide once: refuse `scope === 'household'` for self members
in all four (coherent with D1), or drop the page-level hiding.

### D6 — P2 — Two server actions skip the same-origin check
`settings/merchant-rules/actions.ts:355` (`previewRuleClearAction`), `:552`
(`previewRerunAllAction`). Add the check first; add an ops guard that every exported action in
every `'use server'` module names `isSameOrigin`.

### D7 — P2 — The staged-OCR poll is bound to no owner
`src/app/api/warranties/receipts/stage/[stagingId]/route.ts:35`. Write the uploader's id into the
sidecar and compare.

### D8 — P3 — `mustChangePassword` gate missing on receipt downloads
`src/app/api/warranties/receipts/[id]/route.ts:48`. Add it, matching the three CSV routes.

### D9 — P3 — Unauthenticated `/api/health` leaks version and a raw DB error on 503
`src/app/api/health/route.ts:41-51`. Log server-side; return a fixed body.

### D10 — P3 — `statement-prefill` resolves a form-supplied loan id with no ownership check
`src/app/api/loans/statement-prefill/route.ts:46`. Resolve through the viewer.

**Solid, leave alone:** receipt path handling and `INLINE_MIMES`; the zip reader (declared sizes
summed before inflating, `maxOutputLength`, zip-slip refused); first-run setup race; update egress
pin on exact pathnames; AES-256-GCM with per-purpose HKDF and `scrubSecrets` at every send; session
destruction scoped by user id; no SQL injection surface; both `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` sites static.

## E. Remaining domains

### E1 — P1 — A rules re-run silently un-flags a loan-assigned transfer, moving real money back into spend
`src/lib/categorize/engine.ts:194` (`ELIGIBLE` has no `loan_payments` exclusion, unlike
`REVIEW_WHERE` at `:1839`) and `:339` (`isTransfer: outcome.isTransfer` written unconditionally).
Assigning with "Also mark as a transfer" writes no rule; the row is `is_transfer=1, category NULL,
source none`; re-run clears the flag. Latent in the repo's own fixture. Fix: make the engine's
transfer write one-directional (only ever set true), or add the `loan_payments` exclusion to
`ELIGIBLE`; assert on the flagged row in the existing test.

### E2 — P1 — SimpleFIN advances `last_sync_at` through a partial bridge failure; the automatic path discards `errlist`
`src/lib/simplefin/sync.ts:244` (`markSynced(now)` unconditional), `:249`,
`src/lib/scheduler.ts:241`. One bank down for 12 days on daily sync: the 5-day overlap cannot reach
back, days 1–7 are never fetched, and nothing is logged or notified. Fix: skip `markSynced` when
`errlist` is non-empty; raise `sync_failed` (or at least log) from the auto path.

### E3 — P2 — Deleting a manual transaction untrains Bayes for a split parent
`src/lib/transactions.ts:1373-1375` has no `transactionHasSplits` guard; `clearCategory`'s docblock
explains exactly why one is required. Fix: skip the untrain when the row has splits; hoist the
helper out of `engine.ts`.

### E4 — P2 — Un-flagging a transfer set by a `contains` rule is reverted by the next re-run
`src/lib/categorize/engine.ts:1486, 1522-1525`. The `not_transfer` override is written only when
`matchesCardPattern`; a `contains` transfer rule re-claims the row. Fix: write the override whenever
any transfer rule other than the exact one would still match.

### E5 — P2 — `applyRenameRules` is O(rows × rules) with no per-merchant memo
`src/lib/categorize/engine.ts:2005`. Every other table walker memoizes per merchant via
`ruleAttributor`. Fix: a `Map<merchant, rule|null>` built once per call.

### E6 — P2 — Month auto-close compares a UTC timestamp's date to a local cutoff
`src/lib/month-close.ts:82`: `lastSyncAt.slice(0, 10) >= cutoff`. A 21:00 sync on 2 April in Toronto
stamps 3 April UTC and closes March a day early. Fix: `todayIso(new Date(row.lastSyncAt))`.

### E7 — P3 — `cashflowTrend` default `endMonth` (same as B9).
### E8 — P3 — SimpleFIN import label shows a UTC clock time (`sync.ts:82-84`).
### E9 — P3 — `purgeOrphanReceipts` docblock demands an ordering its signature cannot honour (`receipts.ts:150-162`); the 24 h age guard is the real protection.
### E10 — P3 — `projectedFinishMonth` is unbounded and can render "September 4551" (`goals.ts:95`). Clamp at ~120 months.

**Correct and worth knowing:** RULE R1 holds exactly in `balance.ts`; `not-ended-agreement` passes;
the receipt sweep cannot delete a live statement receipt; the OCR queue cannot retry a bad file
forever; the R4 rule-ownership invariant is genuinely held.
