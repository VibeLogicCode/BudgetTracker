## Overall Impression

A well-built system carrying one under-designed card at its centre. The loan page has the right pieces in the wrong order: the figure the code treats as authoritative (owing today) is smaller and in the wrong face than the stored balance above it, the explanation for a balance that just rose sits below the figure that rose, and the release's headline action opens a form the person cannot see. The single biggest opportunity is to collapse the balance MetricCard into the ledger card, promote "Owing today" to the hero, and show Balance + Accrued = Owing as one visible sum.

## What's Working

1. The money type and colour system is enforced by tests, not aspirational: named scale steps, a display face with tabular numerals for figures, WCAG-measured control borders. In Operate mode brand lives in precise details and this is a real one.
2. `BASIS_LABELS` / `BASIS_HINTS` turn the hardest decision in the product into recognition ("Yearly rate, one twelfth charged each month" plus the loans it usually is), with a documented refusal to guess.
3. The reconcile model: declare the drift, stay correctable, keep the statement, log how far off it was, refuse an undo because deleting one would make the log lie. Three ways in, one way out, nothing writes without a person looking. The preview sentence before commit is the best moment in the product.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Reconcile opens a form the user cannot see and drops focus to body.** The button unmounts on click; the form mounts as a sibling card below the entire ledger table; no scroll, no focus move (editing does get scrollIntoView). A second orphaned copy of the button floats in a bare div. On a two-year loan the visible result of clicking is that the button disappeared. **Fix:** render the form inside LoanLedgerCard under the header action, as Add rule / Add receipt already do on this page, keep the button mounted with `aria-expanded`, focus the first field, close on success and switch the result to `tone="success"`. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`.
- **[P1] One balance, six statements, three numbers, five labels; "Balance" means two things on one card.** MetricCard hero shows the stored balance; the ledger header shows Owing today and Balance (posted); the table's last Balance cell equals Owing today; two more restatements below. Stored balance and posted balance diverge between a due posting and the sweep. **Fix:** delete the separate MetricCard, promote the ledger under the summary card, make Owing today the single `.money-xl` hero, render Balance + Accrued so far = Owing today with a visible relationship, rename the column "Running balance", drop to 3-4 header figures on StatTile's grammar, move lifetime figures to a footer strip, route every direction-sensitive label through `INTEREST_WORDING`. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`.
- **[P1] The reconcile preview mislabels the date of its own estimate.** It reads owing-as-of-today and prints "Our estimate for {asOfDate}", where asOfDate is user-editable and silently overwritten by the PDF reader. A four-week-old statement is compared to today's figure and the "difference" is an artefact. The $50 threshold is noise on a mortgage and alarming on a small loan. **Fix:** compute the estimate at asOfDate (or label it "for today"), make the threshold relative, debounce the live region. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P1] Loans are unfindable from the nav and a dead end from the dashboard, and the dashboard total does not add up.** Five nouns for one destination (Contracts & Coverage / Items / Back to items / Warranties & bills page / Create warranty); the word "loan" appears only inside a closed `<details>`. Neither dashboard loan card links anywhere. LoansCard's total sums owing-today while its rows print the stored balance, so the total exceeds the rows and the only caption explains a different gap. **Fix:** put "Loans" in the nav label and h1 (or give loans their own Planning entry), add kind filter pills, link dashboard rows to the loan, unify the noun, make LoansCard's rows and total the same quantity. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] LoanLedgerCard opts out of the app's own money typography, grid arithmetic, table stacking, hover contract and table markup.** Labels are `text-sm text-muted` not `.eyebrow`; values are body-face with no tabular numerals; 7 children render 3/3/1; money cells are hand-written so `responsive` stacking is unavailable and the six-column table scrolls sideways on a phone while the narrower table below it stacks; rate/basis/day-count live only in a `title`; "Show by month" has no `aria-pressed` and no 44px target; the whole table is nested inside TableWrap's own `<table>`. **Fix:** rebuild the header on StatTile, use `AmountCell` + `responsive`, pass `<thead>/<tbody>` to TableWrap instead of a second `<table>`, replace the title with a disclosure row, size and label the toggle. Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`.

## Minor Observations

- Reconcile success renders `tone="info"` where all five sibling actions on the page use `tone="success"`. One-word fix, real peak-end gain.
- `$` in every ledger cell (~200 on a three-year loan) breaks the optical decimal alignment tabular numerals exist to create. State the currency once.
- Empty strings for null money cells where `Detail` uses an em dash; a blank is ambiguous between zero and not-applicable.
- `duePostings` / `dueAdjustment` are computed and never rendered; the UI never says a posting is owed, which is exactly the window in which the two balance figures disagree.
- Three content widths in two clicks: wide list → default detail → `max-w-2xl` edit.
- Help describes `/warranties` as "seventh in the menu", a positional reference that will rot.
- The cold-start empty state lists four kinds; the app frames five everywhere else (bill is dropped).
- Two `ProgressBar` implementations; the shared one should take a tone override rather than be forked.
- 16 `<th>` without `scope` (11 in notifications-client, 5 in one reports table); the new loan files are fully compliant.
- The rail is genuinely good: three rendered runs, longest-prefix active match, `aria-current`, a fixed mobile panel that locks scroll and returns focus on Escape.

## Questions to Consider

1. Help says three figures sit above the ledger table; the card ships seven. Which document is wrong, and what does it tell you that the person explaining the card could only account for three?
2. The code requires net worth to agree with "the figure printed at the top of the loan's own page" and uses owing-today; the top of the page prints the stored balance. Should the balance MetricCard be deleted and Owing today promoted to the hero, making the comment true?
3. The rail learned in September that a structure in a docblock is not a structure on screen. Why did that lesson stop at the nav?
4. "Loan" is the word the transactions kebab uses, the dashboard card is about, and help spends its longest passage on. Why is it the one word the section that holds loans refuses to print?
5. A statement has one balance column, states its currency once and rules a line before a total. Which parts of "like a bank" were worth keeping: the layout, or the discipline?
